Definitions and calculation notes


Scorecard bucket names: Car Buyer Project Package, September 11, 2026, §6, page 6. Deal Check labels and format: §5, pages 5–6. Add-on wording: §4 and Clip 7. The coach's current instructions supply the response mappings, the $5 reconciliation tolerance and the $2 payment tolerance. A separately titled “twelve lines” table was not located in the available package.


OTD = selling price + dealer add-ons + service fee + tax + title/plate fees − rebates. Net trade = trade allowance − trade payoff. Expected amount financed = OTD − net trade − cash down + products. Do not enter an add-on twice: selling price must exclude the separately entered add-on list.


Payment estimates use an equal monthly amortizing loan with APR as the model rate. A separate timing adjustment uses the entered days for both estimated payment and payment-implied APR. The original 30-day formula is unchanged. A mismatch is a question, not proof of a lender buy rate, markup or legal violation. Loan estimates are unavailable for leases.


Days default to 30 independently on each sheet; whole days from 30 to 120 are accepted. For these estimates, divide days into whole 30-day monthly periods and a remaining fraction. With r = APR / 1200, t = whole periods and f = remaining days / 30, the timing factor is (1 + f × r) × (1 + r)^(t − 1). Estimated payment = original payment × timing factor; extra pre-amortization interest = principal × (timing factor − 1). Total projected interest = all adjusted payments − principal; the separately displayed deferral components are not added a second time. No dates, fees or irregular payments are invented. Exact contract APR needs actual calendar dates and the full schedule.


Coach review starts above 45 days. This is not a legal cutoff: for a transaction of at least one year but less than ten years, Reg Z §1026.17(c)(4)(ii) permits disregarding up to 21 extra first-period days (up to 51 days using a 30-day regular period). Timing differences alone do not establish a violation. Authorities: §1026.17(c)(4) and Appendix J(b)(5)–(6), (c)(1).


Payment attribution uses this fixed order: selling price, add-ons, service fee, tax, title/plate fees, rebates, trade allowance, payoff, cash down, products, any accepted reconciliation difference, APR, term, then first-payment timing. Available timing room holds the New sheet's principal, rate and term fixed. A proposal is not added to achieved Scorecard savings. A final unexplained-payment line preserves a difference in the printed payments.


Cash savings exclude extra cash down and changes in an old debt payoff. Fees corrected includes dealer service fee and title/plate fees. Entered tax and rebate changes reconcile the cash total separately, without creating new Scorecard buckets. The trade-after-tax-versus-cash-offer row is a separate benchmark, not an extra amount added to trade allowance. No state tax credit or product split is guessed.


Net = cash transaction changes + projected interest changes − coaching fee paid. Cash net is also shown separately. Projections assume all scheduled payments with no early payoff or refinancing. When a payment does not reconcile, the interest projection stays labeled as the stated-APR model. A coach chooses the final verdict; the calculations do not establish that the documents are authentic or complete.
